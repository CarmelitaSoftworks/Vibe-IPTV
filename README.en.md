**Vibe IPTV** is a **free and open-source** project (**GPLv3** license) by **Carmelita Softworks**, made of two
native apps built around the same idea: an **installable web app (PWA)** that runs in any browser, and a
**native app for Android and Android TV** written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. It has a tile-based
channel menu, settings organized by sections and **three playback engines** (ExoPlayer, VLC and MPV), so
even the tricky cable-TV channels play.

All the code is out in the open: you can review it, build it yourself, modify it and share it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **It's only a player.** It doesn't include, sell or recommend channels, playlists or streams.
> You add your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes account from a legitimate provider.

📖 **First time?** The [User guide](docs/GUIDE.md) explains step by step how to install it, add lists, set up the guide and use subtitles.

🖥️ **Running your own server?** (NAS, Raspberry Pi, mini PC, VPS, Tvheadend…) Follow [Install on your own server](docs/SELF-HOSTING.md), with Docker, a Linux service or Windows.

## ✨ Features

### ▶️ Playback
- **Three engines on Android** that take over from each other automatically: ExoPlayer (Media3) → **VLC** (LibVLC) → **MPV** (libmpv). If one can't handle a channel, the next one tries; you can also pin one in Settings.
- **Cable-TV formats**: interlaced MPEG-2 video and AC-3 / E-AC-3 / MP2 audio, with automatic deinterlacing.
- **In the browser**: hls.js and mpegts.js, plus on-the-fly **ffmpeg** conversion through the local server when the browser can't play a format.
- **Configures itself for your device**: at startup it detects memory, CPU and decoders. On modest TV boxes (Xiaomi Mi Box / Mi TV Stick, 1–2 GB devices) it turns on **lite mode**: menus without shadows or animations, lighter artwork and lighter video processing.
- **Continue watching on the home screen**: your half-watched movies and series, with their progress bar.
- **Adjustable buffer** (Fast / Balanced / Stable) across all three engines, plus a **learned buffer**: if a channel keeps stalling, the app raises its buffer on its own and remembers it.
- **Stable live channels**: no jumping ahead or flickering, with automatic reconnection if the signal drops.
- Player menu with audio tracks, quality, aspect ratio, speed, technical channel info, favorites, picture-in-picture (PiP) and a channel list without leaving the video.

### 🧭 Navigation
- Big, clear **tile menu**, comfortable with the remote's D-pad on Android TV.
- **Live TV**, **Movies** and **Series** sections, with **categories and subcategories** taken from your list.
- Series grouped by season and episode, and **Continue watching**: movies and series resume where you left off.
- **Search** and **favorites**.
- Animated splash screen in the menu's style, and a welcome screen to add your first list in seconds.
- On Android it opens **in landscape** by default, like a TV.

### 🗂️ Lists & guide (EPG)
- **M3U / M3U8 playlists** from a URL or a file, and **Xtream Codes accounts** (with automatic port and format detection).
- **From a screenshot**: upload the photo or screenshot of your provider's message and the server, username, password, M3U link and guide fill in automatically. Text is read on your device (ML Kit on Android, Tesseract.js on the web): the image is never sent to any server.
- **Multiple saved lists** and quick switching between them.
- **XMLTV program guide** (also .gz-compressed) that **updates itself** when you add a list and when the app opens. Supports merging several guides and a time offset.
- **Grid guide** (Android and web): channels and shows on a timeline, easy to browse with the remote.
- **Catch-up TV** (Xtream): watch shows that already aired from the start, if your account supports it.
- Works with guides generated by the open-source [iptv-org/epg](https://github.com/iptv-org/epg) project.

### 💬 Subtitles
- Searches **several sources at once**: OpenSubtitles (the same database as VLC's **VLSub** extension), SubDL and Podnapisi.
- **Offline AI subtitles** (Android): transcribes Spanish, English or Portuguese audio live with [Vosk](https://alphacephei.com/vosk) (open source). The **AI** build (`vibe-iptv-ia-*.apk`) includes the Spanish model; the regular one downloads it the first time (~40 MB).
- **Automatic subtitles** (on by default): in movies and series, if the audio isn't in your language, it uses the built-in track or finds and loads one from OpenSubtitles and Podnapisi, **no key or account needed**.
- **SubDL built in through its official API**: searches by IMDb ID, downloads just the episode from season packs, flags SDH subtitles and lets you **test your key** in Settings.
- Opens `.zip` files, fixes the encoding of Spanish `.srt` files (Windows-1252), converts `.ass` and lets you **shift the subtitle earlier or later**.

### 🎨 Appearance & settings
- Settings by section (quick, lists & guide, appearance, playback, subtitles, network, startup, data).
- Light theme (default), dark or system, with accent colors.
- It can jump straight back to the last watched channel when the app opens.
- **Themes**: Light, Dark, **Tiles** (grey and green, square corners), **Waves** (purple) and **Custom** (colours, background image, translucent tiles), which can be exported and imported to share it.
- **Console and desktop style themes** (Android): **Pulse** (icon row, the chosen one large with its background image, in blue), **Horizon** (big featured item and rows of tiles with a white border, in green) and **Desktop** (early-2000s blue window, desktop icons, categories in the side panel and a taskbar with a start button). Original design: no logos or assets from any brand.
- **They fit every device**: TV, phone (portrait or landscape), tablet and laptop; on large screens everything scales up in proportion.
- **In Spanish and English**: follows the device language or can be set in Settings → Appearance → Language.

### 🔒 Privacy
- **No accounts, no ads and no telemetry.** Your lists, favorites and settings are stored only on your device ([privacy policy](PRIVACIDAD.md)).
- **Parental control:** with a PIN, adult categories are hidden.
- **Profiles** (Android): favorites and "Continue watching" per person; the kids profile never shows adult content.
- **Recording** (Android): the ● button in the player saves a live channel to *Downloads/VibeIPTV*.
- **Adjustable player controls size** (Settings → Playback).
- **Full remote support**: number keys to jump to a channel, **previous channel**, and a sleep timer (15 to 120 min).
- **Mini player on TV**: the video keeps playing in a corner while you browse the menu or the guide.
- **Multiview**: up to 4 channels at once; the one you pick plays sound.
- **Recently watched**, **search the guide by programme name** and **organize categories** (hide and reorder).
- **Android TV / Google TV home screen row** with your favorites and recent channels.
- **Double tap** left or right to skip back or forward 10 s (phones).
- **Send log** (Settings → About) to report problems, without usernames or passwords.
- **Backup**: export and import lists, favorites, "Continue watching" and settings in one file, e.g. when switching TVs.
- Xtream passwords are hidden from error messages and logs.

## 📥 Installation

### Android / Android TV

**📺 On your TV with Downloader** (Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, TV box): install the *Downloader* app,
allow it to install unknown apps and type the code:

<div align="center">

## **9370754**

or the address `carmelitasoftworks.github.io/Vibe-IPTV/tv`

</div>

Step by step for each kind of TV, plus troubleshooting: **[docs/DOWNLOADER.en.md](docs/DOWNLOADER.en.md)**.

**📱 On your phone:** open **[carmelitasoftworks.github.io/Vibe-IPTV](https://carmelitasoftworks.github.io/Vibe-IPTV/)** and tap *Celular o tablet* (phone or tablet),
or download the APK from **[Releases → test build](../../releases/tag/prueba)**:

| File | For | Short link |
|---|---|---|
| `vibe-iptv-armv7.apk` | Almost every TV, Fire TV and TV box | `…/Vibe-IPTV/tv` |
| `vibe-iptv-arm64.apk` | Phones, tablets and 64-bit TV boxes | `…/Vibe-IPTV/movil` |
| `vibe-iptv-x86_64.apk` | Emulators and some Chromebooks | — |
| `vibe-iptv-universal.apk` | If you're not sure (bigger file) | `…/Vibe-IPTV/universal` |

Allow **“Install unknown apps”** when installing. Requires **Android 7.0 (API 24)** or newer (the MPV engine needs Android 8.0+).
To update, install the new version on top: your lists and settings are kept.

### Browser (PC, Mac, phone)
1. Download the repository (**Code → Download ZIP**) and unzip it.
2. Install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (version 18 or newer).
3. On Windows, double-click `web/iniciar.bat`. On other systems: `cd web && npm install && node server/server.js --open`.
4. To use it from your phone or TV at home, use `web/iniciar-red-local.bat` (or `--lan`).
5. From the browser you can **install it as an app (PWA)**.

### 📱 iPhone and iPad

The web version works in Safari and can be **added to the Home Screen** like an app:

1. On a PC, Mac, NAS or Raspberry Pi at home, start the server for your local network: `web/iniciar-red-local.bat` on Windows, or `HOST=0.0.0.0 node server/server.js` (Docker works too).
2. On the iPhone, open the address the server shows in Safari (for example `http://192.168.1.20:8080`).
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen, with its own icon.

On iPhone, Safari can't play MPEG-TS channels directly: the app first uses the channel's HLS version (`.m3u8`) and, if there isn't one, the server converts it to HLS with **ffmpeg** (included with `npm install`). Picture-in-picture and AirPlay work. If the video starts muted, tap the screen once to turn the sound on (an iOS rule).

To use it away from home, publish the server over HTTPS (for example, a Cloudflare tunnel) and **always with a password** (`VIBE_PASSWORD`). See [Self-hosting](docs/SELF-HOSTING.md).

### Your own server (Docker, Linux, Raspberry Pi, NAS)
```bash
git clone https://github.com/CarmelitaSoftworks/Vibe-IPTV.git
cd Vibe-IPTV/web
docker compose up -d        # http://SERVER-IP:8080
```
Running it as a service, access password (`VIBE_PASSWORD`), HTTPS and how to connect Tvheadend,
Threadfin or xTeVe: **[docs/SELF-HOSTING.md](docs/SELF-HOSTING.md)**.

## 🛠️ Building from source

### In the cloud (nothing to install)
Every change on the `main` branch builds the APK automatically with **GitHub Actions**
([`compilar-apk.yml`](.github/workflows/compilar-apk.yml)). You can also start it by hand from
**Actions → Compilar APK → Run workflow**. The APK is published under **Releases → test build**, and if
something fails the full log is saved to the `build-logs` branch.

### On your computer
```bash
cd android
./gradlew assembleDebug
# APKs in android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/
```
You need JDK 17 and the Android SDK (or just open the `android/` folder in Android Studio).

### Web app tests
```bash
cd web
npm test
```

## 🧱 Tech stack

| Part | Technology |
|---|---|
| Android | Kotlin 2.1, Jetpack Compose, Gradle 8.13 / AGP 8.11, SDK 36 |
| Android playback | Media3 ExoPlayer, LibVLC, libmpv |
| Networking & images | OkHttp, Coil |
| Screenshot reading | ML Kit Text Recognition (Android), Tesseract.js (web) |
| Web | Framework-free JavaScript (ES modules), PWA |
| Web playback | hls.js, mpegts.js, ffmpeg (optional, via `ffmpeg-static`) |
| Local server | Node.js with no required dependencies (proxy, conversion and subtitles) |

### Project layout
```
android/   Native app for Android and Android TV (Kotlin + Compose)
web/       Web app / PWA, local Node.js server and Docker files
web/deploy Sample systemd service, Caddy and nginx configs
docs/      User guide and self-hosting guide (Spanish and English)
i18n/      Spanish → English dictionary shared by the web app and Android
.github/   Automatic APK build
```

## 🤝 Contributing

Contributions are welcome! Open an **issue** to report bugs or suggest ideas, or send a **pull request**
with your improvements. Please keep the project's focus: **a neutral player with no bundled content**
(playlists, channel links or credentials are not accepted).

When reporting a channel problem, **don't post your username, password or full playlist URL**.

Contributing: [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) · Security: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) · Privacy: [PRIVACIDAD.md](PRIVACIDAD.md) · APK signing: [docs/FIRMA.md](docs/FIRMA.md)

**Translations:** the English texts live in [`i18n/en.json`](i18n/en.json). Fix or add a phrase there and
run `node i18n/build.mjs` to update both the web app and Android.
